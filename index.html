About Me & Promotional Ads

The Story, The Drive & The Craft

A look into my creative journey, cross-cultural roots, and official promotional video showcase made for the Graphic Design curriculum.

Hello, I'm Andrei Shin Gutierrez

I was born in the Philippines, but grew up in Hokkaido, Japan with my grandparents until returning to the Philippines at age 11. Living across two distinct cultures shaped my eye for precision, minimalist elegance, and relentless dedication.

“Success isn't inherited, it is seized. The future belongs to those who see the world not for what it is, but for the wealth it can generate.”

— Andrei Shin Gutierrez

Currently studying BS Computer Science (Class 3CS4) at City College of Calamba, I combine technical computational logic with deep visual intuition. Whether developing full corporate brand identities, prototyping interactive mobile experiences in Figma, styling high-end editorial layouts, or editing rhythmic promotional video ads, I believe in creating work that commands attention.
